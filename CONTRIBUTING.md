# 🚀 Contributing to Project "Lunar Cactus"

Welcome! If you are here, you share our vision of a sustainable, eco-friendly fusion energy economy powered by space biomining. The **Opuntia hybrida ramazzottius** framework is an open-source, conceptual bio-hybrid matrix designed for Helium-3 desorption under simulated lunar conditions.

As a purely theoretical framework independently initiated, this project is fully open to global collaboration. We are looking for engineers, bioinformaticians, data scientists, and space enthusiasts to transform these equations into functional digital simulations and real-world bio-blueprints.

---

## 🛠️ Where We Need Your Help (Open Tasks)

We have identified several critical milestones where your expertise can drive this technology forward. Feel free to claim or contribute to any of the following domains:

### 1. 🧬 Genetic & Bioinformatics Modeling
*   **Genomic Architecture:** Digital drafting of the synthetic genetic circuits combining *Opuntia* (CAM-photosynthesis), *Ramazzottius varieornatus* (CAHS and Dsup radiation/cryo protection genes), and *Cupriavidus metallidurans* (lithotrophic pathways).
*   **Protein Folding Simulations:** Computational modeling of gas vesicle protein complexes (GvpA / GvpC) to simulate gas-impermeability against Helium-3 under hydrostatic pressure.

### 2. 🧮 Physics & Hydrodynamics Simulations
*   **Capillary Flow Analysis:** Modeling the selective extraction of microscopic Helium-3 bubbles from fluid mixtures via the root system's xylem channels.
*   **Membrane Stress Testing:** Numerical solutions and finite element analysis (FEA) of the monolayer graphene dome structure sustaining internal atmospheric pressure against a hard vacuum ($P_{\text{vacuum}} = 0$).

### 3. 🎨 3D Visualization & CAD Engineering
*   **Ecosystem Architecture:** CAD modeling of the lightweight protective dome structures and enclosed macro-architecture on the lunar regolith.
*   **Anatomical UI:** 3D animated renders of the biological "sponge" microstructure within the stem parenchyma.

## 📬 How to Get Involved

1.  **Fork the Repository:** Create your own copy of the project to test modifications.
2.  **Open an Issue:** If you spot a mathematical mismatch, a biological optimization, or want to suggest a new feature, open a GitHub Issue to start a technical discussion.
3.  **Submit a Pull Request (PR):** Ready with a simulation script, a 3D model, or text improvements? Submit a PR, and we will review and merge it into the main framework.
